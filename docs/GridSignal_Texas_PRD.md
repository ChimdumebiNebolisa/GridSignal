# GridSignal Texas Product Requirements

## Primary user

County planners, emergency managers, and community resilience staff.

## Product promise

Provide transparent public-data planning signals for structural resilience need and backup feasibility at Texas-county level, with current conditions and source limitations visible.

## Requirements

- Cover all 254 Texas counties where bundled data is available.
- Keep structural need, backup feasibility, operational context, and utility context separate.
- Expose component values, quality, vintage, timestamps, limitations, and missingness.
- Never present heuristic scores as outage predictions or professional advice.
- Support map, search, keyboard-accessible county selection, profile details, and plain-text export.
- Validate data coverage, score configuration, score fields, and reproducibility before release.

## Non-goals

Outage prediction, utility reliability determination, exact service territory, site-specific solar design, household-level recommendations, and a cross-horizon composite score.
